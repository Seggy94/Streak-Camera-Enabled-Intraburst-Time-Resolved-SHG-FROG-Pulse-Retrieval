Streak Camera Enabled Intraburst Time-Resolved SHG FROG Pulse Retrieval

The processes involved in forming bursts of high repetition rate, ultrafast pulses can yield variation in pulse fields within a burst.

To resolve the pulse fields with intraburst time resolution, we use a streak camera instead of a ccd camera in SHG FROG to obtain a spectrogram, instead of a spectrum, for each time delay.

Below is example raw data: a spectrogram for each delay time.

The spectrograms are sliced into time segments, and individual FROG traces are obtained for each time slice. Phase retrieval is performed on each FROG trace, and thus a unique pulse is reconstructed for each time slice.

If a burst's length exceeds the sweep time range of the streak camera, the above measurement is performed for multiple time segments of the burst. Then the burst time segments are stitched together to form a spectrogram of the entire SHG FROG burst for each delay time.
